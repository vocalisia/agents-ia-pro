Publisher FAQ

Who is the Agents-IA.pro audience?

SME leaders, innovation managers, IT consultants and digital agencies — mainly French-speaking (FR, CH, BE, LU, CA). Typical profile: company with 10 to 500 employees looking to deploy a practical AI agent.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. Unlike traditional marketplaces, Agents-IA.pro takes no commission. You only pay for monthly visibility, your sales are 100% yours.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no commitment. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

How is my agent validated?

Our team tests each agent in real conditions before publication. Criteria: demonstrable ROI for an SME, clear documentation, FR/EN support available.

What happens after payment?

Within 48h, our team will contact you to collect your pitch, screenshots, and demo video. Your listing goes live within 5 business days.

Are the links dofollow for my SEO?

Yes, on Bronze/Silver/Gold plans, links to your site are dofollow (SEO juice passed). This is a strong advantage compared to marketplaces that nofollow all their links.

Yes — Stripe handles conversion automatically. Invoicing by Agents-IA.pro (Switzerland), no VAT for the end client (art. 293B CGI exemption for France).
